# SOUND Entertainment — User Manual

## For Visitors (No Account)

### Browsing
- **Home page:** See featured releases, latest 5 songs and 5 videos, trending tracks, albums, artists, and categories.
- **Music:** Click "Music" in the menu to browse all songs. Use the search box and filters (artist, genre, language, album, year) to narrow results.
- **Videos:** Click "Videos" to browse all videos with the same filtering options.
- **Albums:** Browse all albums and click any to see its songs.
- **Artists:** Browse artists and click any to see their biography, songs, videos, and albums.
- **Categories:** Browse by genre (Pop, Rock, Hip Hop, etc.).
- **Search:** Use the search bar in the header for live suggestions, or the full search page for complete results across songs, videos, artists, and albums.

### Viewing Details
- Click any song to see its image, description, audio player, related songs, and reviews from other users.
- Click any video to watch it in the built-in player and see suggested videos and reviews.

## For Registered Users

### Creating an Account
1. Click "Sign Up" in the top right.
2. Fill in all fields (username, full name, address, phone, email, password).
3. All fields are mandatory and validated.
4. Your username must be unique.
5. After registering, you are automatically logged in.

### Logging In
1. Click "Sign In".
2. Enter your username or email and password.
3. Use the demo admin account `admin` / `admin123` for admin access.

### Rating and Reviewing
1. Open any song or video.
2. Use the star rating in the review form to give 1-5 stars.
3. Write your review in the comment box and click "Post Review".
4. Your review appears immediately. You can edit by submitting again.

### Favourites
1. On any song or video page, click the "Favourite" button.
2. The heart fills and the item appears in your dashboard under "Favourite Songs" or "Favourite Videos".

### Playlists
1. Go to "Playlists" from your profile menu.
2. Click "Create Playlist" and give it a name and description.
3. Open a playlist to view its songs and videos.
4. You can rename or delete playlists from the playlists page.

### Dashboard
- Click "Dashboard" from your profile menu to see your favourites, playlists, and recently played history.

### Profile
- Click "Profile" to edit your name, address, phone, avatar, bio, and change your password.

## For Administrators

### Accessing the Admin Panel
- Log in with an admin account (e.g., `admin` / `admin123`).
- Click "Admin Panel" in your profile dropdown.

### Dashboard
- See statistics: total songs, videos, albums, artists, users, comments, categories, and newsletter subscribers.

### Managing Songs
- Click "Songs" in the admin sidebar.
- Click "Add Song" to add a new song with title, description, image, audio, artist, album, genre, language, year, and duration.
- Click the trash icon to delete a song.

### Managing Videos
- Same as songs, but with a video URL instead of audio.

### Managing Albums and Artists
- Add and delete albums and artists with their details.

### Managing Categories
- Add and delete genres and languages.

### Managing Users
- View all users, toggle their role between user and admin, or delete accounts.
- You cannot delete your own account or remove your own admin access.

### Managing Comments
- View all user reviews and delete any inappropriate ones.

### Newsletter
- View all email subscribers.
